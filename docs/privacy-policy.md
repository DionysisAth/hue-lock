# Hue Lock – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 10, 2026_

Hue Lock ("the app") is a game made by nwbn ("we"). This policy explains what
data the app uses and why.

## Data the app keeps on your device

Your progress is saved **only on your device**:
- scores, coins, levels, missions and achievements
- settings
- simple gameplay counters, such as runs played

We have no server, and we do not receive this data.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads
and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:
- your device's advertising ID
- your IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out
- information about your device
- how you interact with ads

How Google uses this data is described in
[Google's policy on partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** the app asks for your consent before showing
  personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in
  **Settings → Privacy options**.
- **iOS:** the app asks for permission before using the advertising
  identifier.
- **Remove all ad tracking:** buy "Remove ads". After that, the app only
  shows ads when you choose to watch a reward video.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store.
We never see your payment details.

## Google Play Games / Apple Game Center (optional)

If you sign in, your scores, achievements and saved progress are stored with
Google Play Games or Apple Game Center. This is how leaderboards and cloud
save work. Google's and Apple's privacy policies apply to that data. You can
play without signing in.

## Notifications

The daily reminder is optional and off by default. It is scheduled on your
device and needs no server.

## Children

Hue Lock is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect data from them.

## Deleting your data

- **On your device:** uninstall the app to delete everything it saved.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset it, or opt out of personalized ads, in
  your device settings.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

## Contact

Questions or requests:
